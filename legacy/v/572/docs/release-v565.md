## MediaFlow v565 Stable

A big update, released together with the MediaFlow app 6.0.0.565.

### Fixes
- **Library History:** the "More · N titles" button no longer covers the whole log entry. It's a normal button next to the title chips again.
- **Automatic Backups:** after a reload the browser asks again for permission to write to your backup folder. Backups used to stop silently; now MediaFlow tells you they're paused and one click allows the folder again.
- **Cloud safety copy:** an older tab can no longer replace a newer safety copy written by the app or another browser. It merges the newer changes first.

### Task progress
- **Task progress finishes at:** Minimum (default) or Maximum. With a 9–13 goal, Minimum shows 100% at 9.
- **When you go over the maximum:** Count past 100% (default, warning colour past the maximum), Warning colour, or Status only.

### Background Processes
- New settings: reopen where you left it or at the default spot; stay where dropped or snap to the nearest edge; minimised icon with progress ring and count, ring, count or plain; hide the icon when nothing is running, or always show it.

### Quicker logging
- **Save & get next task** in the + menu while a log is open, with the XP the log earns.
- **Log any title** (Batch Log) in the + menu and as an always-visible button on the Dashboard, including during a session.

### Titles
- **Each title can have its own unit** (for example "matches" for a game) and its own time per unit. Tasks convert it to the category's unit, so 3 matches of 20 minutes count as 1 session of an hour.
- **Names in every language:** titles keep their English, Romanised Japanese, Japanese, Chinese and other names, filled in by recovery and editable. Choose which name shows globally, per category or per title, or keep a title on its main name.
- **Search finds a title by any of its names**, and recoveries search with every name.
- **Collection cover from a title:** pick a cover from a title in the collection or anywhere in your Library. It follows the title if its cover changes.

### XP
- **XP for everything you do:** every action that changes your account earns XP for its kind, set in Leveling & XP (0 turns a kind off). The same action on the same thing earns once every 5 minutes.
- **Background work XP:** automatic backups, finished recoveries and fresh-episode checks.
- **Away XP:** time when MediaFlow isn't open anywhere earns XP at its own rate (default a quarter of the active rate), credited the next time you open MediaFlow. The same time is never counted twice, across all your devices and the app.
- **Website time keeps counting** while the tab is in the background or you're idle.

### Get MediaFlow
- A new **Get MediaFlow** popup with every way to use MediaFlow: Android, Windows, iPhone / iPad and the web app. It always shows the latest stable app release, puts your device first, and checks whether the website and each of your installed apps are up to date.

### Help
- FAQ entries for everything above.
