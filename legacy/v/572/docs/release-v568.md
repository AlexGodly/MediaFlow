## MediaFlow v568 Stable

Released together with the MediaFlow app 7.1.0.568, the first app release for Mac and Linux.

### Get MediaFlow
- **Mac:** download the app as a .dmg or a zip (macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel). It's unsigned: the first time, open it and choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- **Linux:** download the AppImage or a .tar.gz (64-bit, most distributions).
- Macs and Linux PCs open their own card first, and **Your devices** checks MediaFlow on them for updates too.
- The Mac (Finder) and Linux (Tux) logos take the colour of your theme, like the others.
