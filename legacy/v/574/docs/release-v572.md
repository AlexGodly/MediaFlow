## MediaFlow v572 Stable

### Live sync for everything
- **Changes from your other devices now always reach the website.** A save on the website could skip what the app (or another browser) had saved just before, for good: covers and details found by the app's Recovery, edits made in the app, and so on. Reloading didn't bring them back. That's fixed, and the next time you open the website it reads your whole account once more to pick up anything it missed (your own unsaved changes are kept).
- **Live, like logging:** while the website is open, changes made in the app or another browser appear within a few seconds, and right away when you come back to the tab. If you're in the middle of something (an open editor or popup), they appear as soon as you close it.
- The "Updated with changes from your other device" message no longer pops up every time; changes simply appear.
