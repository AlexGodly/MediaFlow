## MediaFlow v564 Stable

### Themes
- Themes now show **their own colours by default**: your selected theme (or the Dynamic Cover Theme) is no longer replaced by plain light or dark surfaces.
- **Light / Dark** is still there: switch on **Global appearance** in Settings → Themes to apply it to any theme.
- Your account is moved over once; after that the switch is yours. The MediaFlow app (5.1.0.564) does the same, and both pick the same Light / Dark.
